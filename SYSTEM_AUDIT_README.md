# Structural Integrity & System Loss Telemetry

## Overview
This module addresses structural bottlenecks, administrative blind spots, and the systemic gap between high-profile technological growth and ground-level labor welfare. True digital transformation and high-performance computing frameworks cannot operate in isolation from human dignity and legal accountability.

## Key Audit Parameters
1. **Labor Protection & Timely Remuneration:** Eradicating systemic payment delays and sub-contractor exploitation.
2. **Administrative Transparency:** Aligning regulatory enforcement with the foundational principles of justice.
3. **Core Philosophy:** *“Manush ke manush hishebe somman janano”* — Human respect and structural equity take precedence over superficial commercial metrics.
